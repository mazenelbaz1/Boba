Super Sonic

SUPER SONIC IS THE ULTIMATE FORM OF SONIC THE HEDGEHOG, ACHIEVED BY COLLECTING ALL SEVEN CHAOS EMERALDS.

IN THIS FORM, SONIC BECOMES INVINCIBLE, GAINING ENHANCED SPEED, STRENGTH, AND THE ABILITY TO FLY. HIS APPEARANCE CHANGES TO A GOLDEN COLOR, AND HIS EYES TURN RED.

SUPER SONIC IS A SYMBOL OF HOPE AND DETERMINATION, REPRESENTING SONIC'S UNWAVERING SPIRIT IN THE FACE OF DANGER.
